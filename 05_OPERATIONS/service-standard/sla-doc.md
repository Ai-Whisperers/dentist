## 2. FIRST-RESPONSE WINDOW DETAIL

- **Target:** 4 hours (Mon-Sat, 8:00-18:00 PYST).
- **Medium:** Messaging / email / phone — whichever the requester used.
- **Escalation:** if response is >4h and <24h, send a status note: "I'm in the chair / I'll reply at 14:00 with the full quote."
- **Required action on receipt:**
  1. Acknowledge with named contact + 1-line summary + ETA for next real answer.
  2. Auto reply/away message already in place per `08_MESSAGING/templates/messaging-setup-configuration-guide.md`.

---

## 3. BOOKING SLA

- **Individual appointments:** next available slot within 5 business days.
- **Corporate employees:** next slot within 10 business days after employee list received.
- **Second opinion sessions:** target 3-5 business days.
- **On-site screening days:** minimum 7-day advance confirmation.
- **Cancellation / no-show policy:** 24h notice for corporate scheduling. If a partner cancels, reschedule within 10 days.

## 7. BREACH HANDLING

1. **Slip >30% of SLA** on response time → send apology note with ETA prespecified next contact time.
2. **Unscheduled on-site cancellation** → immediate reschedule + concession decision by Ivan.
3. **Proposal delivered after 48h** → reissue within 24h + brief note on reason (clinic pressure, custom schedule).
4. **Monthly reports >6 days late** → notify HR within 24h with exact delivery date + optional phone summary.

## 9. EXCLUSIONS
