## DAILY ROUTINE (Every Day)

### Morning (Before First Patient)
| Time | Action | Duration |
|------|--------|----------|
| 7:30am | Check WhatsApp — respond to overnight messages | 15 min |
| 7:45am | Review today's appointments — confirm reminders sent | 5 min |
| 7:50am | Prepare operatory — materials, sterilization, setup | 15 min |
| 8:00am | First patient OR admin time | — |

### Between Patients
- Check WhatsApp between patients (every 2 hours if busy)
- Log any new contacts in CRM immediately
- Send follow-up messages if due (per timing rules)

### End of Day (After Last Patient)
| Time | Action | Duration |
|------|--------|----------|
| 30 min after last patient | Process WhatsApp — respond to all pending | 20 min |
| | Update CRM — log today's interactions | 10 min |
| | Review tomorrow's appointments | 5 min |
| | Send appointment reminders for tomorrow (if not auto-sent) | 5 min |

### Evening
- Check WhatsApp once more (8pm cutoff — after that, use away message)
- No urgent responses until morning unless emergency

## WEEKLY ROUTINE

### Monday Morning — Weekly Review (30 min)
1. Open CRM → review week's metrics:
   - New contacts: X
   - Appointments booked: X
   - Show rate: X%
   - Hot leads: X
   - Revenue: Gs X

2. Check calendar — what's the week look like?
   - How many appointments?
   - Any second opinions or complex cases?
   - Any follow-ups due this week?

3. Review outstanding actions:
   - Pending follow-ups
   - Complaints or issues
   - Referral thank-yous due

4. Plan the week:
   - Content posting (if doing social media)
   - Outreach activities (one new contact per week)
   - Any supply orders needed

### Friday Afternoon — Week Close (30 min)
1. Log week's revenue in CRM
2. Review: What worked? What didn't?
3. Prepare for Monday's first day:
   - Any materials needed?
   - Any messages to send proactively?

## MONTHLY ROUTINE

### First Day of Month — Monthly Review (1 hour)
1. Financial review:
   - Revenue this month: Gs X
   - Expenses this month: Gs X
   - Net: Gs X
   - Vs last month: +X% / -X%

2. Pipeline review:
   - Total contacts in CRM
   - Conversion rate (contacts → booked)
   - Hot leads (second opinions) — follow up
   - Cold leads — decide: re-engage or archive

3. Operations review:
   - How many patients seen?
   - How many new vs returning?
   - Show rate?
   - Any complaints?

4. Strategy check:
   - What's generating the most leads?
   - What needs adjustment?
   - Any new opportunities?

### Mid-Month
- Review Timbrado validity — renew if expiring
- Check consumables/supplies — order if low
- Send birthday messages to patients with birthdays this month
- Send recall messages (6-month cleaning check)

### Last Week of Month
- Monthly metrics report (open CRM, export data)
- Plan next month's focus
- Send referral reminders to recent patients
- Review referral credit balances (who has credits to use)

## QUARTERLY ROUTINE

### Every 3 Months — Deep Review
1. Financial health:
   - Revenue trend (up/down/stable)
   - Expense ratio (is OpEx under control?)
   - Runway (months of expenses covered)

2. Marketing effectiveness:
   - Which channels producing patients?
   - Cost per acquisition per channel
   - ROI of any paid marketing

3. Operations check:
   - Equipment maintenance done?
   - Supplies vendor relationships good?
   - Any processes breaking?

4. Strategic check:
   - Pricing still competitive? (maybe one price adjustment per year)
   - What's the competition doing?
   - Any new specialist relationships to build?

## WHAT TOoutsource WHEN SCALE

### When to Hire an Assistant (Part-time)
**Trigger:** >15 patients/week AND WhatsApp overwhelming

**What to delegate:**
- Appointment confirmation calls
- CRM data entry
- Basic WhatsApp responses (pricing, scheduling)
- Supply ordering
- Filing

**Keep for yourself:**
- Clinical decisions
- Treatment planning
- Patient follow-up (clinical)
- Complaints
- Specialist relationships

### When to Hire a Dedicated Admin
**Trigger:** >25 patients/week OR multiple days of practice

**What to delegate:**
- Full WhatsApp management (with templates)
- Appointment scheduling and confirmation
- Billing and invoicing
- Patient file management
- Follow-up messages

## KEEPING THE SYSTEM ALIVE

### The Biggest Risk: Inertia
Once launched, it's easy to stop doing the weekly reviews, stop logging to CRM, stop sending follow-ups. This kills the system.

**How to prevent:**
- Friday afternoon ritual: 30 min, non-negotiable, review CRM
- Every patient → CRM entry (no exceptions)
- Every WhatsApp → response or acknowledgment
- Set phone reminders for weekly reviews

### The Second Risk: Overwhelm
Patient care is the priority. If WhatsApp and admin becomes overwhelming, patients suffer.

**Solution:**
- Specific WhatsApp hours (9am-6pm)
- Batch response times (morning + afternoon + evening)
- Use auto-replies for after-hours
- Say no to non-essential tasks
