# DENTAL TELEHEALTH — PARAGUAY TELECHECK FRAMEWORK
> AI research | June 2026 | Human clinical validation required

**Remote patient care** | After treatment follow-up | Source: Market research

---

## Current Digital Dentistry Landscape in Paraguay

Based on expat and clinic research:

- No clinic in the competitive set currently advertises post-operative
  telecheck as a service
- IPEO and Risus handle follow-up via WhatsApp messaging and in-clinic visits
- Some specialists use WhatsApp photo review informally (doctor approves
  photos sent by patient)

Telehealth regulatory status in Paraguay:
- MSPBS has not yet enacted explicit dental-telehealth-specific regulations
- General telemedicine guidance exists; dental is a gray zone
- Most clinics operate tele-advice informally (WhatsApp) without formal
  telehealth licensing
- Risk: informal use is tolerated; formal licensing adds cost

---

## Recommended Approach for Dra. GP

**Phase 1: Structured informal (no license cost)**
- After every surgical episode: patient sends photos on Day 3, Day 7, Day 14
- Doctor reviews in WhatsApp, approves or flags for in-person
- Bot sends "send your photo" prompt at scheduled time
- No formal telehealth consult billed — included in procedure

**Phase 2: Formalize (when revenue supports)**
- Add "telecheck" as a billable service (Gs 150,000–250,000)
- Requires MSPBS registration as telemedicine provider
- Until then, keep as value-add within procedure fee

## Telecheck Protocol (informal)

| Day | What patient sends | What doctor checks | Action if problem |
|-----|-------------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| 3 | Occlusal + healing photos | Swelling/infection signs | Flag in-person in 24h |
| 7 | Progress photo series | Tissue healing | Normal if improving |
| 10 | If sutures present | Suture status | Instructions vs removal |
| 14 | Final healing photo | Final reconciliation | Schedule final |

For non-surgical work (cleanings, restorations): no telecheck needed; use
standard immediate post-op instructions + open WhatsApp line.

---

## Implementation Notes

- Patient-facing: webcam selfie tutorial link in WhatsApp post-op template
- Doctor-facing: Telegram channel for fast photo review
- Privacy: photos processed on personal devices; no third-party upload to
  cloud galleries without patient consent
