# PRICE LIST CARD
## Dra. Gabriella González Pane — Physical Card
**Version:** 1.0 — June 2026

---

## PURPOSE

A wallet-sized card with pricing to hand to patients who ask "how much does it cost?" Saves you from reciting prices verbally and gives them something to take away.

---

## DESIGN

### Size
Credit card size: 85mm × 55mm (standard business card)
