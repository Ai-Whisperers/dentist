# CONTENT STRATEGY
## Dra. Gabriella González Pane
**Phase:** Planning (not building)
**Version:** 1.0 — June 2026

---

## PURPOSE

Create a minimal but effective content presence that:
1. Shows up in search when people look for a dentist
2. Builds trust before they contact her
3. Answers the questions patients are already asking
4. Differentiates her philosophy without being salesy

**Philosophy:** Content is educational, not promotional. Every post should teach something or show something real. Never "visit my clinic" spam.

---

## CONTENT PILLARS

### Pillar 1: Dental Education (60% of content)
The goal: answer questions people are already searching

**Topics:**
- "What happens during a root canal" (vs. what people think happens)
- "Why does my dentist recommend a crown after a root canal"
- "Second opinions in dentistry — when should you get one"
- "The difference between a filling and a crown"
- "Why does dental insurance push certain treatments"
- "What is overtreatment in dentistry"
- "How to know if you actually need that procedure"

**Format:** Text post (300-500 words) + 1-2 images or graphics

### Pillar 2: Philosophy & Approach (25% of content)
The goal: show the human and philosophy behind the practice

**Topics:**
- "Why I became a dentist who plans first" (her story, human, specific)
- "The case I refused — and why" (real case, anonymized, shows values)
- "What a second opinion appointment actually looks like"
- "How I decide what treatment to recommend" (behind the scenes, educational)
- "Why conservative dentistry matters"

**Format:** Text post (400-600 words) — longer, more personal

### Pillar 3: Social Proof (15% of content)
The goal: let patients speak for her

**Topics:**
- Testimonial excerpts (with permission)
- Before/after cases (with permission, anonymized)
- Results from real patients
- Thank-you moments (with permission)

**Format:** Screenshots of messages, short video testimonial (if permission), before/after photos

## PLATFORMS

### Priority 1: Facebook (Expat Groups + Personal Profile)
**Why:** Where the expat community asks for dentist recommendations
**Approach:**
- Post educational content to her personal profile (visible to friends)
- Engage in Facebook groups when someone asks for dentist recommendations
- Never spam — respond to specific questions with helpful answers

**Example engagement:**
> Someone in "Expats in Paraguay Uncensored" asks: "Can anyone recommend a good dentist who won't try to upsell me?"

> Her response (not a sales pitch):
> "I'm a dentist — not posting this as promotion, but if you're evaluating options, feel free to message me. My approach is to plan before I treat, and I don't recommend work I don't think you need. No pressure, happy to just look at your case and give you an honest opinion."

### Priority 2: LinkedIn
**Why:** Professional credibility, English-speaking audience, expats
**Approach:**
- Profile optimized for "second opinion dentist Paraguay"
- Post occasionally (1-2x/month) on dental topics
- Share longer-form thoughts on dentistry/philosophy

**LinkedIn profile optimization checklist:**
- Headline: "Odontología con Criterio | Second Opinions | Asunción, Paraguay"
- Bio: "20 years experience. Planning before treating. Not the fastest — the most careful."
- Location: Asunción, Paraguay
- Experience: Include "Second Opinions" and "Treatment Planning" prominently
- Recommendations: Ask satisfied patients/collleagues (if any)

### Priority 3: Google Business (Reviews)
**Why:** This is the #1 trust signal for local search
**Approach:**
- After every successful patient interaction, ask for a review
- Reviews should mention: "explained everything," "didn't push unnecessary work," "took time"

**Review request template (WhatsApp, after successful visit):**
```
"Hola [NOMBRE]! Gracias again for coming in today. If you had a good experience, I'd really appreciate if you could leave a Google review — it helps others find me when they're looking for a dentist.

Here's the link: [GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

If you'd rather not, no problem at all — just let me know if you have any questions after your treatment!"
```

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't post procedure photos (bloody, scary — bad for brand)
- Don't post memes or jokes (wrong tone for a professional medical practice)
- Don't post about prices as the main hook (undercuts premium positioning)
- Don't post more than 2x/week (spammy, she'll burn out)
- Don't engage in arguments or negative reviews publicly (handle privately)
- Don't create a separate "business" Facebook page (personal profile + groups is enough at launch)
