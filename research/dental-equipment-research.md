## 1. Portable / Mobile Dental Equipment

### What is available

**Portable dental units** are self-contained systems that integrate:
- Air compressor (silent, oil-free)
- High/low speed handpiece controls
- Suction (saliva ejector + high volume)
- Water supply (bottled or built-in)

### Key Brands

| Brand | Country | Positioning | Portable Line |
|-------|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **Aseptico** (GO Ultra) | USA | Professional portable | Yes — top tier |
| **DNTLworks** | USA | Mobile dentistry specialist | Yes — since 1986 |
| **Avtec Dental** | USA | Portable/mobile units | Yes |
| **NSK** | Japan | Premium handpieces + portable | Some portable units |
| **StarDental** | USA | Portable delivery systems | Yes |
| **BEING** | China | Mid-range portable | Yes |
| **Sino Dental Group** | China | Budget portable | Yes |

### Price Range — Portable Units (2024-2026)

| Type | Price (USD) | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Basic portable unit** (chairless, compressor only) | $500–$900 | AliExpress/B2B |
| **Portable unit + chair combo** | $1,500–$3,500 | Chinese brands |
| **Mid-range portable system** (Aseptico, DNTLworks) | $4,000–$8,000 | USA made, professional |
| **High-end portable** (A-dec portable, NSK portable) | $8,000–$15,000 | Premium features |

### Specific Products Found

- **Aseptico GO Ultra (AEU-350)**: ~$4,000–$6,000 — currently on 4-week backorder due to demand
- **4H Portable Dental Unit (AliExpress)**: ~$755 (built-in compressor, strong suction)
- **QL2028III Dental Chair**: ~$2,300–$3,600 (new, China-made)

### Pros vs. Traditional Installation

| Portable | Traditional Fixed |
|----------|-------------------|
| ✅ Lower upfront cost ($3K–$15K vs. $25K–$100K) | ✅ More robust, higher throughput |
| ✅ Can start in rented space without construction | ✅ Seamless integration with plumbing |
| ✅ Mobile — can do home visits, events | ✅ No equipment weight/transport concerns |
| ✅ No construction permits needed | ✅ Quieter (compressor can be in another room) |
| ✅ Ideal for Phase 1–2 while testing market | ✅ More stable for high-volume practice |
| ❌ Limited to 1-2 operatories | ❌ Requires construction, permits |
| ❌ Smaller water reservoir, fills needed | ❌ Fixed location |
| ❌ Less powerful suction for heavy procedures | ❌ High upfront investment |

**Recommendation for Dra. Pane**: Given her Phase 3 plan and current model transition, portable equipment makes sense for **Phase 1 rented space** to minimize risk. Traditional installation if/when she secures a permanent location with volume justifying it.

## 2. Chinese Dental Equipment

### Major Chinese Manufacturers

**Top Chinese dental chair/equipment brands (2025):**
1. **Sino Dental Group** — large state-affiliated exporter, full product line
2. **BEING Dental** — mid-range, well-built, popular in LATAM
3. **Huge Dental** — budget line
4. **Roson** — dental chairs, popular on Amazon/eBay
5. **Foshan Qizhen** — budget equipment
6. **Run Dental** — top 10 Chinese export brand
7. **ZIANN Medical** — high-end Chinese chairs with AI integration

### Quality vs. German Premium

| Aspect | German (KaVo, Dentsply, A-dec) | Chinese Mid (BEING, Foshan) | Chinese Budget |
|--------|--------------------------------|----------------------------|---------------|
| **Price** | $15,000–$50,000/chair | $2,500–$6,000/chair | $800–$2,000 |
| **Durability** | 20+ years | 8–15 years | 3–8 years |
| **Precision** | Highest | Good | Basic |
| **Noise** | Very quiet | Moderate | Louder |
| **Parts** | Global support | Regional support | Hard to get |
| **LATAM presence** | Distributors | Some distributors | eBay/Alibaba only |

**Key comparison points:**
- **NSK** (Japan) is often considered "bridge" between German quality and Chinese pricing
- German chairs use high-torque, noise-reduction technology (valuable for patient experience)
- Chinese manufacturers have "won wide recognition among global dental practitioners for excellent cost performance" — but this is variable by brand
- Chinese high-end brands (ZIANN, Run Dental) now integrate AI, touch screens, memory functions

### Where to Buy in Paraguay / Import

- **Ubuy Paraguay** (ubuy.com.py) lists dental equipment with 110V/220V options for PY
- **Sino Dental Group** sells through distributors, some ship to Paraguay
- **Direct import**: AliExpress, Alibaba — but consider:
  - Shipping cost (heavy equipment = expensive freight)
  - Import duties in Paraguay (~10-20% depending on classification)
  - No local warranty
  - 220V/50Hz requirement must be specified

### AliExpress Options

- Portable dental units: $500–$2,000
- Dental chairs: $1,500–$4,000
- Portable X-ray machines: $800–$2,500
- micromotor portable: $200–$800
- Full portable chair+unit combo: $2,000–$5,000

**Warning**: AliExpress dental equipment is generally **budget/prosumer grade**. For a premium positioning practice like Dra. Pane's target, this may not be appropriate for core equipment. Acceptable for backup or secondary portable setup.

Industry standard for a US startup: $250,000–$500,000 (but this includes high labor costs, large space, multiple operatories). In Paraguay, costs are significantly lower.

### Dra. Pane's Scenario (Financial Model reference)

From FINANCIAL-MODEL.md — Scenario C (Integrated Specialist Practice):
- Equipamiento básico: **Gs 80-150M** (~$11,000–$21,000 at 7,000 GYN/USD)
- Total inversión: Gs 200-400M (~$28,500–$57,000)

This aligns with a **mid-range basic setup** — a single operatory or two, quality but not premium German.

### Renting Equipped Space

**Advantage**: Drastically lower upfront cost
- No equipment purchase (just consumables + small tools)
- Move-in ready
- Test market before committing to purchase

**Typical equipped consultorio rental in Asunción**: Gs 8-15M/month (~$1,100–$2,100/month)

**Break-even calculation**:
- If buying equipment at $20,000, renting equipped saves ~$1,500–$2,000/month → pays back in 10-16 months
- But: renting = no equity in equipment, terms may be restrictive
