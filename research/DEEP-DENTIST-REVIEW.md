**E.A.S. (Empresa por Acciones Simplificada):**
- Created by Ley 6.089/2018
- Single shareholder allowed
- Simplified administration
- Register at: eas.mic.gov.py
- Lower setup/maintenance costs vs S.A./S.R.L.

### 4.2 Habilitación Sanitaria — Step by Step

**Authority:** Dirección de Establecimientos de Salud, MSPBS

**Required steps:**
1. Register professional with MSPBS (Registro Profesional)
2. Prepare architectural plans (scale 1:50 for spaces up to 1,000m²)
3. Obtain Certificate of Approval of Plans from municipal body
4. Submit habilitation application with all documents
5. Pay tariff per Resolución S.G. N° 106/2013
6. Pass inspection
7. Receive Habilitación Certificate

**Physical Requirements:**
- Minimum area: 9m²+ for consultorio (dental operatories need more)
- Reception/waiting area
- Bathroom with disability accessibility (Ley 4.934/2013)
- Dental operatory with space for equipment, suction, compressed air
- Sterilization area (physically separated)
- Storage for supplies/equipment
- DEA (Automated External Defibrillator) required (Ley 5.578/2016)
- Waste disposal contract with certified company (Resolución S.G. 750/2002)
- If X-ray: ARN authorization required

**⚠️ KEY INSIGHT:** If she rents an ALREADY-HABILITATED space, this entire process is already done. This is the #1 reason to rent equipped rather than build.

### 4.5 Patient Data Protection — NEW LAW

**Ley 7.593/2025 (November 2025) — Protección de Datos Personales:**

Key provisions:
- Health data = **datos personales sensibles** (explicit protection)
- Patient consent required for data use
- Patients have right to: access, rectification, deletion, portability
- Habeas data action if not honored within 30 days
- 72-hour breach notification requirement
- Confidentiality obligation persists after relationship ends

**Can she take the patient database?**
- **NO** — patient lists are typically clinic property
- **YES** to patient requesting their own records (transfer to new dentist)
- **YES** to anonymized aggregate data for professional purposes
- **Recommended:** Never copy identifiable database. Let patients choose to follow her.
