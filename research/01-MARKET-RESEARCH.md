## 2. INSURANCE MODEL IN PARAGUAY

### How it works (from audio):

The Círculo Odontológico Paraguayo sets "arancel" (fee schedule):
- Arancel SOP for reference: ~Gs 1.500.000 for some procedures
- Insurance companies use these high reference prices to:
  1. Charge patients 50% of arancel as "coverage"
  2. Pay dentists only 10-15% of what they collect
  3. Keep the spread as profit

### Problem identified:

- Patient pays Gs 500.000/month to insurance
- Insurance collects Gs 500.000 but only pays clinic Gs 75.000 (15%)
- Clinic pays dentist Gs 60.000 for procedures worth Gs 550.000
- **The system is designed to extract value at every level**

### Key insight from audio:
"No existe un todólogo" — even specialists are underpaid through this system
