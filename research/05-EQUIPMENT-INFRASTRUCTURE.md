## 4. PORTABLE/MOBILE OPTIONS

### From audio:
"Hay un coso móvil ahora, un cosito móvil... tu compresor lo único tenés que tenerlo lejos con el ruido"

### Portable dental units exist:
- Complete portable setups
- Include compressor, handpieces, light
- Cost: $2,000-8,000 USD
- Good for satellite locations

### Chinese equipment:
"En China hay una versión que no te hace destruir tu piso"

From research:
- Alibaba has dental equipment
- Quality varies widely
- Can save 50-70% vs. German brands
- Risk: quality control, warranty, parts
