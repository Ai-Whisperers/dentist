# Research: Consultorio Rental Options in Luque, Paraguay

## Summary
Luque has an emerging medical corridor around the CIT (Centro Internacional de Convenciones) / Traumatocenter area, with consultorios available from Gs. 1.2M–5M+ monthly. Fully equipped dental consultorios are scarce in Luque specifically — most equipped dental spaces are in Asunción proper. Prices in the Gs. 2.5M–5M range get you properly located medical office space near the main health cluster.

## 7. Requirements for Dental Practice Space (Paraguay)

Based on general knowledge + search findings:

### Minimum requirements for dental consultorio:
- **Water:** Conexión de agua potable (potable water connection)
- **Drainage:** Desagüe adecuado para equipos odontológicos
- **Electricity:** 220V preferably, adequate amperage for dental equipment
- **Space:** Mínimo ~30m² recommended for single chair; ~50m² for two operatories
- **Aire acondicionado** (mandatory for equipment longevity and patient comfort)
- **Bioseguridad:** Eliminación adecuada de residuos biosanitarios
- **Habilitación municipal** required for operation

### Para consultorio odontológico:
- Registro en el Ministerio de Salud Pública y Bienestar Social
- Habilitación de la Itaipá (?) depending on location
- Seguro de responsabilidad civil profesional
- RUC (Registro Único del Contribuyente) active

## 9. Recommendations for Dra. González Pane

1. **Zona CIT is the highest value location** — medical cluster near Traumatocenter, good for patient perception and referrals. Gs. 5M+ gets equipped space there.

2. **Luque centro** offers best value for empty/semi-equipped spaces at Gs. 1.2–1.5M — but requires investment in dental equipment installation.

3. **For a start-up practice:** Consider Gs. 2.5–3.5M range consultorios in Asunción (Bella Vista, Villa Aurelia) which come equipped and have established patient traffic.

4. **Share model:** Some dentists in Asunción offer part-time rental (2–3 days/week) — search Facebook groups for "alquilo consultorio por día/por turno."

5. **Negotiate:** Most rents are negotiable with 6–12 month contracts, especially outside Asunción centro.
