## 7. MEDICAL TOURISM VS. LOCAL EXPATS

### Two different markets:

**Medical Tourists** (dental tourism):
- Come specifically for dental work
- Price-sensitive (comparison shopping)
- One-time or occasional
- Need international marketing
- High transaction value

**Local Expats** (ongoing care):
- Live in Paraguay permanently
- Need regular dental care
- Value relationship/trust
- Willing to pay for quality
- Recurring revenue

### Recommendation for Dra.:
**Focus on LOCAL EXPATS first** — it's her existing network, no marketing needed, recurring revenue, higher lifetime value.
