## 1. BUSINESS STRUCTURE OPTIONS — PARAGUAY

### From search result: jurisprotec.com.py

Three main options for entrepreneurs in Paraguay:

| Structure | Description | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **E.A.S.** (Empresa por Acciones Simplificada) | Newest, simplified, low capital requirement | Small businesses, solo entrepreneurs |
| **S.R.L.** (Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada) | Partners, minimum 2 members | Small groups, professional practices |
| **S.A.** (Sociedad Anónima) | Traditional, requires 3+ directors | Larger businesses, investors |

### For Dra.'s Practice — Recommendation:

**E.A.S. is likely best** because:
- ✅ Can be single member (she doesn't need partners)
- ✅ Low minimum capital
- ✅ Simplified administrative requirements
- ✅ Faster to establish
- ✅ Flexibility for solo practice

### From blog "Conviene abrir una EAS":
"La estructura EAS presenta varias ventajas notables para los emprendedores en línea en Paraguay, incluida la facilidad de creación y los requisitos operativos y administrativos simplificados."

## 2. PROFESSIONAL REQUIREMENTS — DENTISTRY

### From facmedicine.com forum:

**To work as dentist in Paraguay:**
1. Must have dental degree (Odontología) recognized by Ministerio de Salud Pública
2. Registration with professional association (Círculo Odontológico)
3. RUC for tax purposes
4. Individual vs. company structure decisions

### Minimum requirements:
- Professional license (Matrícula profesional)
- Sanitary habilitation for consultorio
- Municipal operating license
- Tax registration (RUC)

## 3. HABILITACIÓN SANITARIA — CONSULTORIO

### What is habilitación:
Official authorization from health authorities to operate a healthcare facility.

### Requirements (from Argentina models as proxy — Paraguay similar):

Based on research, general medical consultorio requirements include:
- Physical space requirements (m2 minimum)
- Bathroom facilities
- Sterilization area
- Proper ventilation
- Equipment specifications
- Fire safety
- Accessibility

### For dental specifically:
- Dental chair and equipment specifications
- X-ray equipment (if applicable)
- Sterilization protocols
- Waste disposal
- Emergency equipment

### Steps in Paraguay (estimated):
1. Apply to DIGESA or municipal health authority
2. Submit floor plans and equipment list
3. Inspection by health officials
4. Initial authorization
5. Annual renewal

**NOTE**: If renting an already-habilitated space, this is already done.

## 4. RUC & TAX OBLIGATIONS

### RUC (Registro Único del Contribuyente):
- Tax identification number
- Required for any business activity
- Dra. likely already has one from her work at Odontología 3

### Tax system for professionals:

| Tax | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| IVA | 10% | Standard rate |
| IRE/IRP | Variable | Income tax — varies by structure |
| Municipal license | Variable | Based on city and revenue |

### E.A.S. tax implications:
- Profits taxed at entity level (E.A.S. rate)
- Owner draws salary or profits
- Simpler than S.A. for tax filing

### From Paraguay Context skill:
- IRE / IRP — corporate and personal income tax (IRACIS was replaced)
- IVA — 10% standard, 5% for basics
- Timbrado — required for invoicing

## 6. PROFESSIONAL ASSOCIATIONS

### Círculo Odontológico Paraguayo:
- Professional guild for dentists
- Sets recommended fee schedules (aranceles)
- Provides continuing education
- Members must pay dues

### Benefits of membership:
- Professional legitimacy
- Access to aranceles for insurance negotiations
- Networking with other dentists
- Some legal protections

**Recommendation**: Join or maintain membership even as independent practitioner.

## 9. KEY LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR TRANSITION

### From employment to independent:

1. **Non-compete clauses**: Check employment contract
   - Some contracts prohibit working in same area for X months
   - Generally difficult to enforce in Paraguay

2. **Client solicitation**: Don't actively recruit from current employer
   - Can say "I'm starting my own practice"
   - Can't use employer's resources/patient list

3. **Professional reputation**: She has 13 years — her reputation is HERS
   - Patients will find her
