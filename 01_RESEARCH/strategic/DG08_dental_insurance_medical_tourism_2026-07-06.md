# DG08 — Dental Insurance Acceptance + Medical Tourism (PY)

> **For Gaby.** Insurance panel participation + medical tourism opportunity for PY dental clinics. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY dental insurance market**: dominated by IPS (public), Sancor, Mapfre, Paligmed. Most mid-market clinics don't accept insurance directly — patients pay out-of-pocket + submit for reimbursement. **Medical tourism** is a real opportunity: dental care in PY is 50-70% cheaper than US/EU. Argentina + Brazil + now Paraguay positioning strongly.

## 14 data points

### Insurance panel participation

1. **IPS (public)** — covers basic dental (profilaxis, exodoncias, urgencias). Reimbursement rates low (~30-50% of SPOyP arancel)
2. **Sancor Seguros** — major PY health insurer. Includes dental in some plans (Salud Segura). Reimbursement ~60-80% of SPOyP
3. **Mapfre Paraguay** — "Trebol Salud Global Exclusive". International coverage including dental
4. **Paligmed PY** — local, covers basic dental
5. **BlueCross PY** — affiliate of BlueCross BlueShield. Limited dental coverage
6. **Asismed** — prepaid medicine, often used by expats. Includes basic dental
7. **Decisión clínica**: aceptar todas las aseguradoras vs selectivamente. Trade-off: panel participation reduces autonomy but increases patient flow

### Cross-border insurance billing

8. **CDT codes (US standard)** — required for US insurance claims
9. **ICD-10 codes** — required for medical necessity documentation
10. **Direct billing** to US insurers possible via companies like Insurance billing services, e.g. **Careington** or **Aetna dental network** (intl expansion)

### Medical tourism opportunity

11. **PY dental tourism**: dental care 50-70% cheaper than US. Argentina + Brazil already established
12. **Common treatments sought**: implants ($700-1,000 vs $3-5K US), full mouth restoration ($5-10K vs $30-50K US), cosmetic veneers ($250 vs $1,500 US per tooth)
13. **PY advantages**: Spanish-speaking dentist, no visa required for most tourists, growing tourism infrastructure
14. **Disadvantage vs Costa Rica/Colombia**: less established "dental tourism" brand, fewer all-inclusive packages

## How Gaby could enter dental tourism

- Website in English (Spanish/English/Portuguese)
- "Dental vacation" packages: 5-7 days in PY + procedure + recovery time
- Partnerships with Asunción hotels + tourism operators
