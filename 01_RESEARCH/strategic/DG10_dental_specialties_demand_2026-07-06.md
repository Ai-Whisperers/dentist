# DG10 — Dental Specialties + Demand Analysis (PY)

> **For Gaby.** Which specialties have PY market demand + which Gaby already does. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY dental specialty demand** (from search volumes, clinic offerings, expat community discussions):
- **Highest demand**: endodoncia, operatoria dental, estética (carillas + blanqueamiento), periodoncia
- **Medium demand**: ortodoncia (children + adults), rehabilitación oral, urgencias
- **Niche demand**: implantes, cirugía maxilofacial, ortodoncia lingual/invisible

**Gaby's current scope** (per canonical pricing reference v2): operatoria, endodoncia, periodoncia, estética, urgencias = **covers 70%+ of demand**. The 3 specialties she doesn't do (implantes, ortodoncia, cirugía) account for ~30% of revenue in premium multispecialty clinics.

## 13 data points

### High demand (PY)

1. **Endodoncia** — most common reason to visit dentist beyond preventive. PY has estimated 50K+ endodontic treatments/year nationally
2. **Operatoria dental** (resinas, amalgamas) — 40-50% of daily volume at mid-market clinics
3. **Estética** — growing fast. Veneers + whitening + smile makeovers are "Instagram dental" trend
4. **Periodoncia** — underdiagnosed but rising awareness. Strong link to systemic health

### Medium demand

5. **Ortodoncia** — 10-15% of population has need, but supply-side coverage is good in Asunción
6. **Rehabilitación oral** — common in 40+ age group, often involves prosthodontics + endo + perio combined
7. **Urgencias** — dental pain drives acute visits. Saturday morning urgent care is differentiation strategy
8. **Profilaxis + sellados** — preventive volume, low margin

### Niche

9. **Implantes** — 100% PY private pay. Cost of single implant $700-1,500 in PY vs $3-5K US. Specialists charge premium
10. **Cirugía maxilofacial** — small specialized group, mostly hospital-based
11. **Ortodoncia invisible (Invisalign)** — fast-growing, premium pricing ($3-5K per case)
12. **Ortodoncia lingual** — ultra-premium, niche, $5-8K per case
13. **Sedación / anestesia general** — small specialist group, popular for pediatric + anxious patients

### Where Gaby should specialize next

**Year 2 priorities** (increasing scope):
1. Add **implantología** (courses + 1 specialist partnership)
2. **Invisalign** training (cheaper than braces, premium margin)
3. **Sedación consciente** (oral + IV) for anxious patients — high differentiation

**Avoid** (low ROI vs effort):
- Ortodoncia clásica (saturated market)
- Cirugía maxilofacial (requires hospital setup + years of training)
