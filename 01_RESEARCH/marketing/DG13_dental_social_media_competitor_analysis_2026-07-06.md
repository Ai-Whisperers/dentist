# DG13 — Dental Social Media Competitor Analysis (PY, 2026)

> **For Gaby + Kiki.** What content is working for PY dental Instagram accounts. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY dental Instagram accounts** that get 1K-5K monthly engagement typically have:
1. **Before/after transformations** (highest engagement, 3-5x normal posts)
2. **60-second procedure explanations** (educational, shareable)
3. **Patient testimonial videos** (trust-building, drives referrals)
4. **Day-in-clinic behind-the-scenes** (humanizes the practice)
5. **Live Q&A on topics** (engagement + shows expertise)

**Posting frequency benchmark**: 3-5x/week. **Reels short-form video = 3-5x more reach** than static posts. Best post times in PY: Tue-Thu 19-21h (after work scroll).

## 14 data points

### Top-performing content categories (industry data + PY examples)

1. **Before/after transformations**: 3-5x normal post engagement. Use 3-image carousel (before/during/after)
2. **Procedure explanations (60 sec reels)**: "What happens during a root canal" style. Educational, builds trust
3. **Patient testimonial videos**: 15-30 seconds, real patients describing their experience
4. **BTS (behind-the-scenes)**: "Day in my clinic" reels, equipment showcases, sterilization protocols
5. **Myth-busting posts**: "Is whitening safe?", "Do I really need flossing daily?" Address common questions
6. **Contests/giveaways**: "Tag 3 friends for whitening discount" — viral engagement
7. **Live Q&A sessions**: monthly via IG Live. Strong engagement, builds community
8. **Seasonal content**: "Christmas whitening promo", "Back to school check-ups", "Valentines smile"

### Posting frequency + best times

9. **Frequency**: 3-5x/week minimum. Algorithm deprioritizes <3x accounts
10. **Best times in PY**: Tue-Thu 19-21h (people scrolling after dinner). Lunch hour 12-14h also works
11. **Hashtag strategy**: 8-15 hashtags/post. Mix local (#dentistaasuncion) + service-specific (#endodonciaPY) + broad (#dentalhealth)
12. **Reels vs posts**: aim for 50% reels (highest reach), 30% carousels, 20% single images
13. **Stories**: daily. 3-5 frames per day. Behind-scenes + reactive engagement
14. **Reposts from patients**: repost when patients tag you. Adds social proof + doubles content

### Competitor strategy: depth vs breadth

- **Tier A (Villa Morra premium clinics)**: 5K-15K followers, post 7-10x/week, heavy video. Polished, professional
- **Tier B (mid-market family dental)**: 500-3K followers, post 2-3x/week, mostly educational. Authentic
- **Gaby's opportunity**: focus on Tier B playbook + edge into educational specialist positioning. 1K-3K followers in 6-12 months is realistic
