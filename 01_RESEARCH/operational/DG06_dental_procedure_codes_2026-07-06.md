# DG06 — Dental Procedures, ICD-10, + Insurance Codes (PY)

> **For Gaby.** Standard procedure codes for dental practice billing + cross-border insurance. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY dental billing**: no formal CPT or ICD-10 mandate for outpatient clinics (only required for IPS). Most PY clinics use SPOyP arancel descriptions for invoicing. For **international patients** (medical tourism) or **cross-border insurance billing**, **CDT codes (Current Dental Terminology)** are the standard — used by most US/EU insurers.

## 15 data points

### SPOyP arancel categories (the PY standard)

1. **1. Radiología** — radiografías periapicales, seriadas, panorámicas
2. **2. Prevención** — sellados, profilaxis, fluorización
3. **3. Operatoria** — resinas, amalgamas, coronas estéticas/plásticas/metálicas
4. **4. Endodoncia** — pulpotomía, pulpectomía, tratamiento de conductos
5. **5. Cirugía** — germectomía, exodoncias, frenectomías, cirugía de tejidos blandos
6. **6. Clínica del bebé** — asesoramiento a padres, prenatal
7. **7. Anestesia general** — +50% sobre presupuesto normal

### Common CDT codes (ADA, used by US/EU insurers)

8. **D0150** — Comprehensive oral evaluation (~$80-150 USD)
9. **D1110** — Adult prophylaxis (cleaning, ~$90-150 USD)
10. **D2391** — Resin-based composite, 1 surface posterior (~$150-250 USD)
11. **D2740** — Crown porcelain/ceramic (~$800-1,400 USD)
12. **D3330** — Endodontic therapy, molar tooth (~$700-1,200 USD)
13. **D7240** — Removal of impacted tooth, completely bony (~$300-600 USD)

### ICD-10 dental codes (for medical billing)

14. **K02** — Dental caries
15. **K04** — Diseases of pulp and periapical tissues (incl. pulpitis, necrosis)
