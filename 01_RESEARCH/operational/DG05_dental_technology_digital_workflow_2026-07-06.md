# DG05 — Dental Technology + Digital Workflow (2026)

> **For Gaby.** Modern dental practice technology that improves patient experience + margins. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**The "digital dental practice" stack that PY mid-tier clinics are adopting in 2026**: digital impressions (intraoral scanner) + CBCT 3D imaging + same-day crowns (CEREC/mill) + digital patient communication. For Gaby's "anti-anxiety + planning-first" positioning, the **highest-ROI tech** is the **intraoral scanner** (replaces uncomfortable alginate impressions, ~$15-25K USD) + **CBCT 3D x-ray** ($60-100K new, $20-40K refurbished) for complex cases.

## 14 data points

### Intraoral scanner (replaces goopy impressions)

1. **iTero Element 5D** (Align Technology) — $25-40K USD new, $12-20K refurbished. Most popular globally
2. **3Shape TRIOS 5** — $20-35K USD. Best accuracy
3. **Medit i700** — $15-25K USD. Korean, best value for money
4. **Carestream CS 3700** — $20-30K USD. Good for orthodontics
5. **Open dental scan savings**: 5-10 minutes per patient vs 15-20 min for impressions + storage costs (no physical models)

### 3D imaging (CBCT)

6. **Sirona Orthophos SL** — $80-120K USD new. Industry standard
7. **Vatech Green CT** — $40-60K USD. Mid-range, popular in PY clinics
8. **3D imaging indications**: implants, orthodontics, endodontics, oral surgery. Not essential for routine dentistry but valuable for specialists

### Same-day crowns (CAD/CAM)

9. **CEREC Primescan + Primemill** — $80-120K USD. Sirona system. Same-day crown in 90 minutes
10. **3Shape TRIOS + Roland mill** — $40-60K USD total. Mid-range alternative
11. **Same-day crown value**: charges 20-40% premium ($400-700 USD vs $300 lab-made), saves 2nd visit, premium patient experience

### Patient communication

12. **Dentrix Patient Communicator** — automated appointment reminders + recall. $50-200/mo
13. **WhatsApp Business** (free) — most PY clinics use this instead of SMS. Better open rates
14. **Online booking (Calendly + website integration)** — $8-16/mo. Younger patients prefer this over phone
