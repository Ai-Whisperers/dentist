# DG15 — Expert Interview Agenda: 3 PY Dentists for Market Validation

> **For Gaby.** 30-min conversations to validate market positioning + pricing. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Recommendation**: Gaby conduct 3 informal conversations with PY dentists in different tiers:
1. **Tier A premium dentist** (Villa Morra, multispecialty) — learn what works/doesn't at premium pricing
2. **Tier B mid-market dentist** (Mburucuyá area) — Gaby's direct competitor; build relationship, learn pricing pressures
3. **PY dental association leader** (SPOyP or Círculo de Odontólogos) — understand regulatory + reputation landscape

**Expected total time**: 4-6 hrs (1 hr prep + 3x 45-min calls + 1 hr analysis)

## 14 data points

### Interview 1 — Tier A Premium Dentist

1. **Who**: 1 dentist at a Villa Morra multispecialty (e.g., Bañados Dental, DentaCare Premium)
2. **Ask**: What makes premium pricing work? What does the patient expect?
3. **Topics**: Anti-anxiety protocols, technology investments, staff structure, marketing budget
4. **Goal**: Validate Gaby's premium positioning insights + learn luxury market patterns
5. **How to ask**: Kiki reaches out via reception desk, 30-min coffee chat offer

### Interview 2 — Tier B Mid-Market Dentist

6. **Who**: 1 dentist in Mburucuyá/Barrio (Gaby's direct competitive area)
7. **Ask**: How does pricing pressure work? What's the typical patient acquisition cost?
8. **Topics**: Marketing spend, refund/quality disputes, repeat patient ratio, expansion challenges
9. **Goal**: Reality-check Gaby's pricing + identify what's worked/failed in the tier she's entering
10. **How to ask**: Kiki calls 5 clinics, asks for 30-min conversation. Coffee or virtual.

### Interview 3 — PY Dental Association Leader

11. **Who**: President or board member of SPOyP or Círculo de Odontólogos del Paraguay
12. **Ask**: What's the PY dental landscape + emerging trends? Any regulatory issues I should know?
13. **Topics**: New regulations, industry pain points, training programs, ethics standards
14. **Goal**: Get institutional perspective + possibly get invited to industry events for networking

### Interview prep checklist

- **Question list**: 5-7 questions per interview, not more
- **Note-taking**: Gaby takes notes; don't record unless permission given
- **Incentive**: offer reciprocal information (Gaby's anti-anxiety approach could be of interest to other dentists)
- **Follow-up**: send thank-you note + offer to buy coffee next time

### What to do with the insights

- Create 1-pager per interview with key takeaways
- Cross-reference pricing + positioning insights with existing DG01-DG10 research
- Adjust Gaby's messaging + pricing strategy based on reality-check
- Build ongoing relationships (might lead to partnerships, referrals, etc.)
