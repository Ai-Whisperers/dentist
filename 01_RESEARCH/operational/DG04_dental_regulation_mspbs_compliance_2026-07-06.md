# DG04 — PY Dental Regulation + MSPBS Compliance

> **For Gaby.** Regulatory requirements for opening + operating a dental clinic in Paraguay. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY dental clinic requires**: MSPBS habilitación (Gaby has reg 3618 vigente), Municipalidad commercial permit, SENATUR classification (only if offering accommodation — N/A for Gaby), RC professional insurance (the bloqueante), RUC (Gaby 1375421-1 active). Annual cost of regulatory compliance: ~Gs. 500K-1.5M (~$67-200 USD) in renewals + inspections. Major risk: **operating without MSPBS habilitación** = clinic closure + fines.

## 14 data points

### MSPBS (Ministerio de Salud Pública y Bienestar Social)

1. **Ley 879/1981 Código Sanitario**: foundation law requiring "condiciones del establecimiento" for health services
2. **Habilitación MSPBS**: required for any health-related establishment. Process: 30-60 days, includes inspection + paperwork. Cost: Gs. 200K-500K
3. **Renewal**: every 2-3 years depending on jurisdiction. Cost similar
4. **Gaby's status**: MSPBS 3618 vigente (confirmed via Cuestionario A 6 jul 2026)
5. **Personal médico requirement**: each practitioner must be individually registered (Gaby has it)
6. **Establishment inspection requirements**: sterilization equipment, waste disposal protocol, fire safety, accessibility

### Municipalidad de Asunción

7. **Commercial permit (Permiso Comercial)**: required for any commercial activity. Cost Gs. 300K-800K/year depending on area + size
8. **Mburucuyá zone**: residential area. Need "compatible use" classification for a dental clinic. Sometimes requires "uso de suelo" approval (1-2 month process)
9. **Signage permit**: building exterior signage requires separate permit. Cost Gs. 100-300K
10. **Waste management**: requires agreement with municipal waste collection or private disposal service for biological waste (Biosafety norm MERCOSUR)

### Tax + labor

11. **IVA** (5-10% on services): dental services in PY are 5% IVA typically (lower than 10% general). Verify with contador
12. **IPS** (social security): required for employees. Gaby as owner can opt in or stay private
13. **Aguinaldo**: 12th-month salary paid in June + December (50% each). Mandatory for employees
14. **Salario mínimo**: Gs. 2.798.309/month (~$370 USD) for domestic workers. Assistant dental = Gs. 3-4M/month typical

## Risks + opportunities

- **Operating without habilitación**: Gs. 5-10M fine + clinic closure (3-6 month re-application)
- **Hygiene protocol violation**: Gs. 1-5M fine per inspection
- **No insurance = personal liability** (see seguro-responsabilidad-civil-odontologica-guia.md)
