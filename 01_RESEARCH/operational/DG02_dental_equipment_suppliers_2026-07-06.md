# DG02 — Dental Equipment Suppliers + Capex (PY market)

> **For Gaby.** Dental equipment capex planning for Mburucuyá consultorio. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Full dental equipment capex for a single-sillón consultorio in PY**: $8K-25K USD depending on whether you buy new vs refurbished + branded vs Chinese. The sweet spot for Gaby: **1 new mid-range sillón (~$5-8K) + refurbished accessories (~$3-5K) = $10-13K USD** total equipment. Key PY suppliers: **Dental Guaraní SA** (PY, longest track record) + **DVD Dental** (regional) + import from BR/AR.

## 12 data points

### Sillón dental (the centerpiece, biggest cost)

1. **Dental Guaraní SA** (Asunción, https://dentalguarani.com.py/) — local PY distributor. Models seen: AP027, AP028, A001 (with ultrasonido), CAPRI LUXE FULL. Prices not publicly listed (request quote)
2. **DVD Dental** (regional, https://www.dvd-dental.com/) — Spanish brand distributor, ships to PY. Brands stocked: KaVo, Dentsply Sirona, XO, A-DEC, Tecno-Gaz, Swident, Simple&Smart. Mid-range Sirona Intego ~€15K-25K (~$16-27K USD)
3. **KaVo Estetica E30** — entry-level KaVo, ~€8K-12K (~$9-13K USD)
4. **Refurbished market** — Asuncion has 2-3 dealers of refurbished dental chairs. $3-6K USD. Often includes installation + 6-mo warranty

### Specific equipment categories + PY pricing

5. **Autoclave 16L** (YoyJoy brand, Dental Guaraní) — sterilization, ~$800-1,500 USD
6. **X-ray intraoral sensor** (Xpect Vision, dental Guaraní) — digital radiography, $2-4K USD
7. **Ultrasonido** (built-in or separate) — $500-2K USD
8. **Amalgamator / composite kit** — $300-800 USD
9. **Curing light (LED)** — $200-600 USD
10. **Scaler (cavitron)** — $500-1,500 USD

### Software + IT

11. **Practice management software** (Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, ABELSoft, SiGePY) — $100-500 USD/mo. ABELSoft (Brazilian) + SiGePY (Paraguayan) are most common locally
12. **Digital x-ray software** (Sidexis, VixWin, Romexis) — $500-2K USD perpetual or $50-200/mo subscription
