# DG11 — Dental Pricing Experiments + A/B Testing (PY market)

> **For Gaby + Kiki.** How to test pricing post-launch + iterate. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Dental pricing is sticky + local** — once you publish a price, patients anchor to it. But you CAN run experiments on:
1. **Bundles** (consulta + limpieza + radiografía) at fixed price
2. **Package pricing** (e.g., 5 sesiones endodoncia, full-mouth rehab)
3. **Display order** (premium procedures visible vs hidden)
4. **Promotional offers** (first visit discount, family discount)
5. **Insurance vs cash differential**

Rule: never discount publicly without anchoring; never raise price without warning existing patients.

## 13 data points

### Pricing experiments to run

1. **Bundle test A**: Consulta + profilaxis + 2 radiografías at 480K (vs separate 250+325+200 = 775K). Tests "perceived value"
2. **Bundle test B**: 2x consulta + treatment plan at 550K (vs 600K separate). Tests "premium commitment"
3. **Cash discount**: 10% off for paying upfront in cash (lowers credit card fees)
4. **Family discount**: 2nd family member consulta at 50%
5. **First visit**: Free consulta if friend referral. Track conversion to paid treatment
6. **Package deal**: Complete rehabilitation at 10-15% discount vs per-procedure
7. **Anchoring display**: Show premium procedure (implante $700) BEFORE basic procedures. Patients anchor high, accept mid-tier pricing more easily
8. **Time-of-day discount**: Off-peak consultations (mid-morning Wed) at lower price
9. **Annual plan**: Pay annual fee upfront for Gs. 2M = 2 profilaxis + 2 consultas + discounts on treatments
10. **Payment plans**: 3-6 month plan for treatments > Gs. 2M. Increases conversion from inquiry to treatment

### How to measure

11. **Conversion rate**: track inquiries → consultations → treatments → completed treatments
12. **Revenue per consultation hour**: Gaby's metric. Industry benchmark $40-80 USD/hour
13. **Patient lifetime value (LTV)**: avg 3-7 visits per patient over 5 yrs. Industry benchmark $1,500-3,000 USD LTV

### Rule for raising prices

14. **Existing patients**: maintain old price for 6-12 months. "Grandfather clause" builds trust
15. **New patients**: can charge new pricing. Announce it via website + clearly
16. **Avoid raising across-the-board**: instead, add new service line at new price; wait for old services to churn
