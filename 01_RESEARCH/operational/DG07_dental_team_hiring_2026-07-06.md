# DG07 — Dental Team Hiring + Roles (PY market)

> **For Gaby.** Hiring plan for her single-sillón consultorio expansion. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**PY dental clinic core team** for Gaby's expansion phase: 1 dentist (Gaby herself) + 1 asistente dental (full-time) + 1 receptionist (part-time, can be shared with another dental office) + 1 hygienist (optional, Year 2). **Total monthly payroll**: Gs. 8-12M (~$1,067-1,600 USD). **Hard-to-fill roles**: bilingual EN/ES assistant (current PY supply is small), hygienists (PY has very few formally trained).

## 14 data points

### Role-by-role breakdown

1. **Dentist (Gaby)** — owner-operator. Sets the tone, defines specialty positioning. Already in place.
2. **Asistente dental** — full-time. Helps with 4-handed dentistry, sterilization, patient prep. Salary: Gs. 3-4.5M/month (~$400-600). Hard to find bilingual
3. **Receptionist** — part-time (4 hrs/day) or shared with another clinic. Salary: Gs. 1.8-2.5M/month pro-rated ($240-333)
4. **Higienista dental** — optional Year 2. Allows Gaby to see more patients + delegate cleanings. PY has very few. Salary: Gs. 3-4M ($400-533). Often trained in BR or CO
5. **Contador** (accountant) — outsourced. Gs. 500K-1M/month ($67-133) for monthly bookkeeping + annual tax filing
6. **Abogado** (lawyer) — outsourced retainer. Gs. 200-500K/month ($27-67) for occasional consults
7. **Cleaning service** — for consultorio. Gs. 800K-1.2M/month ($107-160), 2-3x/week
8. **Marketing assistant** — could be part-time freelance. Gs. 1.5-2.5M/month ($200-333)

### PY labor law specifics

9. **Aguinaldo** (13th month) — paid 50% in June + 50% in December. Mandatory for all employees
10. **Vacaciones** — 30 working days after 1 year of service, payable +1 day/month after
11. **IPS** (social security) — 16.5% employer + 9% employee contributions. Both parties pay
12. **Jubilación** — retirement age in PY: 60 years (women), 65 years (men)
13. **Termination notice** — 30 days for <5 years, 90 days for >5 years
14. **Salario mínimo** (2026) — Gs. 2.798.309/month (~$370 USD)
