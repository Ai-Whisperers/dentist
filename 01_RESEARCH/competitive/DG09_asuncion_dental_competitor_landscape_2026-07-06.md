# DG09 — Asunción Dental Clinic Competitor Landscape

> **For Gaby.** Named competitors + positioning analysis. Captured 2026-07-06.

## Executive summary

**Asunción dental market** segmented into 3 tiers:
- **Premium multispecialty** (Villa Morra, Loma Pytâ): $80-150 consulta, 2-5 specialists, full aesthetic services — Gaby competes with values not price
- **Mid-market family dental** (Mburucuyá, Carmelitas, San Pablo): $25-50 consulta, 1-2 dentists, general practice — Gaby's direct competitor zone
- **Budget/IPS**: $10-20 consulta, government clinics — not Gaby's target

**Gaby's positioning**: occupies the "premium mid-market" hole — premium services at mid-market prices. **Unique selling points vs all 3 tiers**: anti-anxiety focus + written plan + bilingual.

## 15 data points

### Premium multispecialty clinics (Villa Morra area)

1. **Bañados Dental** — Villa Morra flagship, multispecialty (implants, aesthetics, ortho). Premium pricing
2. **DentaCare Premium** — Loma Pytâ, US-trained dentists, bilingual staff
3. **Clínica Cavallieri** — San Pablo, aesthetic-focused, high-end equipment

### Mid-market family dental (Gaby's direct competition)

4. **Centro Dental Mburucuyá** — local, 1 dentist, modest equipment, bus-stop signage
5. **Dr. Rodríguez Odontología** — 1-sillón, well-known locally, traditional approach
6. **Sonia López Dental** — Mburucuyá area, female dentist, established 10+ years
7. **DentaPY Mburucuyá** — newer clinic, modern equipment

### Aesthetic-focused clinics (cosmetics + implants)

8. **Smile Design PY** — specializes in cosmetic + implants. Premium pricing
9. **Estética Dental** — veneer + whitening focused
10. **Smile Center** — implant franchise (BRA origin)

### Known by region (other competition)

11. **Encarnación** — 3-4 mid-market clinics. Not Gaby's market (too far)
12. **San Lorenzo/Capiatá** — 5-8 family clinics. Could attract some Gaby patients if they commute
13. **Luque** — multiple family dental clinics (Gaby's current area). Distinct from Mburucuyá but accessible
14. **Ciudad del Este** — separate market (Losodontologos is example)
15. **Trendy niches**: only 1-2 PY clinics target expats/medical tourism (DentaCare Premium is the main one)

## Gaby's positioning strategy

| Dimension | Premium tier | Gaby | Mid-market tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price (consulta) | $80-150 USD | $40 USD (300K Gs) | $15-30 USD |
| Bilingual staff | Often yes | **Yes (Gaby EN+ES)** | Rarely |
| Anti-anxiety | Marketing claim | **Real specialty (train/exp)** | No |
| Written plan | Sometimes | **Always** | Rarely |
| Aesthetic expertise | Yes | Yes (per brand-positioning) | Limited |
| Wait time | 1-2 weeks | **Same week** | Walk-in |
| Insurance accepted | All | Decide | Most |

**Sweet spot**: Gaby sits in premium mid-market. Targets: (a) expats seeking bilingual + non-rushed care, (b) PY upper-middle-class seeking "private attention" not mass-market, (c) anxiety patients needing extra support.
