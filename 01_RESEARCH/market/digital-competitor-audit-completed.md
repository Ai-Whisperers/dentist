## 6. PROJECT LAUNCH SEQUENCE (AI RECOMMENDATION)

Phase 1: Foundation (Week 0–2)
- Register domain + hosting
- Set up WhatsApp Business number
- Create 3 GBP photos now (clinic exterior by day, treatment room, doctor headshot)

Phase 2: Digital ground presence (Week 2–4)
- Launch website (single-page → full 5-page)
- Claim + optimize GBP — fill every field: hours, services, attributes (wheelchair, languages, appointment URL)
- Set up Instagram + Facebook (brand accounts)
- Upload 15 GBP photos (before/after, clinic shots, equipment)

Phase 3: Authority content (Week 4 onward)
- Weekly Instagram Reel: "1 pregunta, 1 respuesta" format
- 1 GBP post per week (offer / tip / appointment slot)
- Patient review SMS/WhatsApp within 48h of appointment

Phase 4: Paid amplification (Week 6+)
- Meta ads targeting Asunción 25–55, health interests, Spanish + English
- GBP boosted posts for "impresiones en Asunción" coupon
- Test Messenger → WhatsApp bridge ads
