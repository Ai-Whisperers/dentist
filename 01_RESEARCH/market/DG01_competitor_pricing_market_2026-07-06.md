## Executive summary

Gaby's canonical pricing (300K consulta, 450K segunda opinión, 800K planificación compleja) positions her **above average** in the PY mid-market but **well below premium** (multispecialty clinics in Villa Morra charge 500-800K consulta). Her pricing aligns with SPOyP's official aranceles (which the consumer market respects as "fair"). The opportunity is the **premium niche** of "anti-anxiety + planning-first + bilingual" that has no direct PY competitor.
