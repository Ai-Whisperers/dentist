## Executive Summary

This report maps 11 categories of dental equipment/space arrangements in Paraguay beyond bare-space rental. The **lowest-risk option remains the equipped San Lorenzo consultorio (Gs 3M/month)**, but multiple alternative models exist depending on Dra. GP's capital position and operational preferences.

**Key Findings:**

- Most equipped consultorio rentals are concentrated in Asunción/Central — online listings limited
- No formal equipment-leasing companies in Paraguay — vendor financing is the alternative
- Professional networks (COP, referrals) drive most arrangements
- Imaging referral services available in Asunción
- Software SaaS at $25-50 USD/month available

## 2. Shared Clinic Arrangements

### Models Available in Paraguay

| Model | Description | Prevalence |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Time-slot rental** | Rent by hour/day in equipped clinic | Limited but exists |
| **Associate practice** | Revenue share (%) with senior dentist | Common |
| **Pop-up days** | Pre-booked days in another's clinic | Occasional |
| **Assistant-sharing** | Split assistant costs + schedule | Rare |

### How It Works

**Associate Model Example:**

- Senior dentist provides: chair, assistant, supplies
- New dentist pays: 25-40% of collections OR fixed daily rate
- Typically: 2-3 days/week to start
- Builds patient base before full investment

### Contact Points

- **COP (Círculo Odontológico del Paraguay):** Professional referrals network
- **WhatsApp groups:** "Odontólogos Paraguay" — active practitioner community
- **Dental schools:** UNA dental gradsnetwork

| Alternative | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Vendor financing** | Most Asunción suppliers offer installment plans |
| **Import + local finance** | Higher cost butMore selection |
| **Bank credit** | Generic SME loans (higher rate) |

### Local Supplier Contact

From equipment research, suppliers to contact:

- **Dental04 Paraguay** — used equipment, may offer rent-to-own terms
- **Local Asunción dealers** — contact via dental supply distributor networks

### Vendor Financing Terms (Typical)

- 20-30% down payment
- 12-36 month installments
- Interest rates: 18-36% APR (Paraguay market)
- Equipment serves as collateral

## 7. Sterilization Services

### Paraguay Reality

| Option | Availability | Practical Assessment |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Central sterilization services** | Not commonly available | Hospitals don't offer externally |
| **Clinic sharing** | Possible | Associate with another clinic |
| **Self-autoclave** | **Recommended** | Essential — do not skip |

### Recommendation

**Purchase basic autoclave** (Gs 3-8M) rather than depend on external service. This is standard of care in Paraguay — patients expect sterilization protocols.

### Autoclave Cost Reference

- Basic tabletop: Gs 2,500,000-4,000,000
- Mid-range: Gs 4,000,000-7,000,000
- Premium: Gs 7,000,000-12,000,000

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Best fit** | Testing market, minimal upfront |
| **Monthly cost** | Gs 3-8M/month |

| **Equipment equity** | None |
| **Flexibility** | High — can relocate |

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|

| **Upfront required** | 20-30% down payment |

| Factor | Assessment |
