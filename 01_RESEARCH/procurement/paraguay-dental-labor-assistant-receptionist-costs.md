## Estimated MVP Staffing Cost at Dra. GP

| Role | Qty | Est. monthly (Gs) | USD equiv |
|------|-----|-------------------|-----------|
| Dental assistant | 1 | 1,800,000 | ~$231 |
| Front desk / receptionist | 1 | 1,500,000 | ~$192 |
| Social security (IPS) employer contribution | — | ~30% of wages | variable |
| **Total wage cost** | | ~4,560,000 | ~$585 |

Paraguayan employer payroll burden is notably higher than gross wage because
of mandatory social security (Instituto de Previsión Social) plus workplace
insurance and other statutory contributions; exact rate varies by contract
type and registration option.

## Hiring Channels

- Facebook Marketplace (dentist assistant positions commonly posted)
- Dental schools (UNA, Universidad Central) — internship pipeline
- LinkedIn — limited for assistant-level roles in PY
- Employee referral — cheapest CAC for this role type

---

## Risk Flags

- No official minimum wage for dental assistants; expect negotiation
- Bilingual requirement (Spanish + English) adds premium (~20–30%)
- Labor market is small: word-of-mouth turnover
- Remote-only administration isn’t realistic; assistant must be in-clinic
