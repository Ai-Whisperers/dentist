## PART 5 - RISKS AND GOTCHAS

1. Habilitacion timeline: 30-60 days if not pre-habilitado. San Lorenzo and Almate are pre-habilitado.
2. 220V/50Hz compatibility: Specify for Paraguay on all imports. Brazilian equipment is native.
3. SIFEN electronic invoicing mandatory for new tax contributors (since 2024-2025). Fines 50-100 percent of undeclared IVA.
4. Aguinaldo (13th month): 8.33 percent extra salary cost per employee per month.
5. No formal equipment leasing in Paraguay - vendor financing only, 18-36 percent APR.
6. Lab fees negotiable at volume: Ask for 10-15 percent discount after 3-6 months.
7. Ley 7593/2025 data protection: Use cloud software with encryption (DenPro compliant).
8. Pagopar link via WhatsApp reduces no-shows 30 percent+. Send treatment plan + link.
9. Anesthesia is highest cost item - track cartridge usage (avg 1.5-2 per restoration).
10. Doctoralia takes 3-6 months to drive leads. Start month 1.

PHASE 1 SETUP (Lowest Risk):
- Rent San Lorenzo equipped consultorio: Gs 3M/mes all-incl
- Buy consumables from Andres H. Arce / Dental Guarani
- Lab: Alta Gama (Asuncion)
- Software: DenPro (Gs 299k/mes) + Pagopar + Bancard
- Marketing: Lean (DIY) for first 3 months
- One-time: Gs 11M | Monthly OpEx: Gs 8-13M | Break-even: 20-30 patients/mes

KEY NUMBERS:
- Materials per restoration: Gs 28-50k (5-12 percent of price)
- Lab fee per crown: Gs 400-700k (12-20 percent of price)
- Per-procedure margin: 72-92 percent
- 6-month runway needed: Gs 50-80M savings
- EAS registration: Gs 1-1.5M
- Pagopar MDR: 3-5 percent
- Bancard MDR: 3 percent

PHASE 2 (After Validation):
- Buy equipment: Gs 100-150M
- Bare space: Gs 3-4M/mes
- Total Phase 2 capital: Gs 130-185M

PROHIBITED:
- Dont rent non-habilitado space (MSPBS 30-60 day delay)
- Dont skip SIFEN invoicing (fines 50-100 percent IVA)
- Dont buy equipment before validating demand
- Dont hire full staff before 20+ patients/mes
- Dont ignore aguinaldo in budget
