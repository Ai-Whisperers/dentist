## Part 6: Lab Fee Structures

### Typical Models in Paraguay

| Model | Description | Prevalence |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| **Fixed fee per unit** | Lab quotes flat rate per crown/bridge | Most common |
| **% of treatment** | Lab takes 25-35% of total fee | Less common in PY |
| **Monthly credit** | Net-30/60 terms, settle monthly | Used by Alta Gama |
| **Rush surcharge** | +25-50% for <48hr turnaround | Common |

### Materials Markup Pattern

Typical flow:
1. Dentist sends Rx to lab
2. Lab quotes lab fee (not % of final price)
3. Dentist marks up 2-3x for patient price
4. Patient pays dentist, dentist pays lab

## Part 7: Outsourcing to China/Asia — Reality Check

### Pros
- Much lower unit costs (50-70% savings)
- Good for high-volume budget cases
- Implant bars, frameworks

### Cons
- 30-45 day turnaround (shipping + customs)
- Customs delays unpredictable in PY
- Quality variable
- Communication barriers
- Limited recourse on defects

### When It Makes Sense
- Long-span frameworks
- Implant bars
- Budget-conscious multiple units
- Cases with built-in buffer time

### Recommended Asian Labs (Common in LATAM)
- Shenzhen dental labs (Alibaba)
- Dental Direkt (Europe/China hybrid)
