## TIER 3 - SHARE WITH OTHER DENTISTS

These are ways to share resources with other dentists without buying.

### 3.1 Associate/timeshare model (CONFIRMED)

How it works: Senior dentist rents you their chair 2-3 days/week. You pay 25-40% of collections to them.

How to find:
- COP (Circulo Odontologico del Paraguay) referral network
- Personal contacts via existing dental school alumni
- WhatsApp group Odontologos Paraguay
- Multi-specialty clinics looking for associated dentists
- Odonto Excellence, Dental Valencia (UNA corridor clinics)

### 3.2 Dental franchise time-share (NEW - June 2026)

Dental Paraguay franchise model offers:
- Dia rental (1-day access to their clinic)
- Semana rental (1-week access)
- Temporal (short-term access)
- Uses their infrastructure (chair, X-ray, etc.)

Action: Contact Dental Paraguay to ask for pricing.

### 3.3 Informal equipment lending via WhatsApp/FB groups

Active groups:
- Odontologos Paraguay (Facebook)
- Odontologos del Paraguay (WhatsApp)
- Various regional groups

Common lends: surgical motor for the weekend, special endo files, handpieces for a day
