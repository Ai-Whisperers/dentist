### Expat Profile (Paraguay)

**Typical expat in Asunción:**
- American/European retiree or remote worker
- USD income (Social Security, pension, remote salary)
- 45-70 years old
- Lives in Asunción, San Lorenzo, or Luque
- Speaks some Spanish (often limited initially)
- Has private health insurance or self-pays

**Expat income bracket:**
- Lower tier: $1,500-3,000/month (Social Security)
- Upper tier: $3,000-8,000/month (pension, remote work)
- Premium: $8,000+/month (business owners, executives)

**Dental significance:**
- At $3,000+/month income, Gs 400-500k ($50-65) for a restoration is essentially nothing
- These patients are comparing prices to US/Davos, not local market
- Loyalty is to quality + trust, not price

## EXPAT DENTAL CARE BEHAVIOR

### From expat forum analysis:

**Key forum thread themes:**

1. **"Need dentist recommendation in Asunción"**
   → Multiple threads, always active
   → Word-of-mouth is primary driver
   → Price is secondary to trust

2. **"How much do dentists cost in Paraguay?"**
   → Typical answer: "Gs 100-200 for cleaning, $100-300 for more complex work"
   → USD pricing because expats convert
   → Some pay USD, some pay local currency

3. **"Is dental care good in Paraguay?"**
   → Mixed responses — some had great experiences, others had overtreatment
   → No trusted brand/universal recommendation

4. **"Dentist who speaks English"**
   → Very common question
   → Limited supply of bilingual dentists

## EXPAT DENTAL SPENDING PATTERNS

### From Forum Data + Market Research

| Procedure | Expat Willingness to Pay | USD equivalent |
|-----------|------------------------|---------------|
| Cleaning | Gs 150-250k | $20-32 |
| Filling (resin) | Gs 400-600k | $50-77 |
| Root canal | Gs 800k-3M | $100-195 |
| Crown | Gs 2-4M | $260-515 |
| Implant | Gs 3-5M | $390-645 |

**Key insight:** Expat price sensitivity is much lower than local market. At USD 3,000+/month income, Gs 400k = $51 = less than a tank of gas in the US. The perceived "value" of quality dental care is extremely high relative to cost.
