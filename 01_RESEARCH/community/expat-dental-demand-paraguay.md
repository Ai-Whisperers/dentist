## Key Chemical / Demand Signals

### Expat blogs are now actively reviewing Paraguayan dental clinics
- **"Best Dentists in Paraguay 2026"** on expatsetle.com is a full ranked
  comparison (6+ clinics, pricing, implant costs) — updated April 2026.
- This signals organic demand from English-speaking expats who are keeping
  track of dental options as a group.

### Uncertainty about "good" dentists remains high
- Expat.com threads show the same question recurring since 2016: "Which dentist
  in Asunción is good for English speakers?"
- Among the old threads, IPEO is the one consistently named in English.
- Threads show pain: trust anxiety, pricing embarrassment ("gringo tax"), lack
  of insurance for expats.

### "Dental tourism" framing is already established
- thingstodoinparaguay.com offers a "Dental Vacation" package with
  "Hollywood Smile" positioning.
- Private clinic prices cited: 50–70% cheaper than US.
- Most cited cost: implant (surgery + crown) ~$1,135 USD at Risus clinic.

   bundled pricing.
