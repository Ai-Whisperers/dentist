## WHAT PATIENTS SAY THEY WANT (vs. What They Actually Choose)

### What patients SAY they want:
- ✅ The best quality materials
- ✅ A dentist who explains everything
- ✅ Second opinions when unsure
- ✅ Conservative treatment (not overtreatment)
- ✅ Clear pricing upfront

### What they ACTUALLY choose (observable behavior):
- ✅ The fastest appointment available
- ✅ The one their insurance covers
- ✅ The cheapest option they can find
- ✅ The one their friend recommended

**The gap:** Patients say they want honesty and quality, but price/convenience often wins. This is why "no overtreatment" must be COMBINED with convenience (scheduling, location, communication) — not just philosophy.
