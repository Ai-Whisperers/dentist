## NOTES ON SOURCE QUALITY

### High Confidence Sources (Official/Government)
- Government portals (SET, MSPBS, MIC)
- Professional association (COP)
- Legal frameworks (BACN laws)

### Medium Confidence Sources (Industry/Research)
- Dental clinic websites (competitor pricing)
- Dental tourism aggregators
- Market research reports

### Lower Confidence (Needs Validation)
- Community forum discussions (Reddit, Facebook groups)
- Classified listings (Clasipar) — prices change rapidly
- Unofficial blog posts

### Always Verify
- Current fee schedules (direct contact with providers)
- Rental prices (call directly)
- Legal requirements (consult lawyer)
