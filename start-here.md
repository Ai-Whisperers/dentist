This is a business launch kit for Dra. Gabriella González Pane to open a premium private dental practice in Luque, Asunción, Paraguay.

**The goal:** Move from the current insurance-volume model (earning Gs 60k per restoration while the market pays Gs 400-550k) to a value-based private practice.

---

## THE CORE PROBLEM

| | Insurance Model | Private Model |
|---|---|---|
| Value generated per restoration | Gs 550k | Gs 550k |
| What she actually receives | **Gs 60k** | **Gs 400-550k** |
| The gap | Gs 490k stolen by the system | She captures the value |

**She generates Gs 550k of value. She gets Gs 60k. That's the problem.**

---

## THE OPPORTUNITY

The market supports Gs 400-550k for restorations. She's currently charging Gs 60k. The gap is 7-9x.

**Gs 400-550k is not premium pricing** — it's upper market standard. She earns it.

---

## THE 3 OPTIONS

| Option | What | Risk | Investment |
|--------|------|------|-----------|
| **A — Upsell** | Premium sessions inside current clinic | Low | Near zero |
| **B — Parallel** | Private practice alongside current job | Medium | Gs 1-2M |
| **C — Full Exit** | Leave and open own clinic | High | Gs 200-400M |

**Recommended: Option B (Parallel) — start private practice while keeping current income**
