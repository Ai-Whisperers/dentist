## PRICING PHILOSOPHY

**Why Gs 350-500k for restorations?**
- Below premium tier (Boccia, IPEO charge Gs 500-700k)
- Above discount chains (Gs 180-300k)
- Market standard + quality positioning
- Expat patients see this as essentially free vs. US prices

**Never discount publicly** — if a patient asks for discount:
1. Offer payment plan (Pagopar 6-12 cuotas)
2. Offer to prioritize treatment by urgency (don't do elective work at discount)
3. "Este es el precio justo por el trabajo que hago"
