## LUQUE DEMOGRAPHICS

- **259,705 residents** — 3rd largest city in Paraguay, part of Greater Asunción
- **152 km²**, density ~1,708/km² — highly urban, commuter-connected
- Universidad Nacional de Asunción (UNA) main campus is in San Lorenzo, ~4–6 km from Luque
- **~40–45k students + faculty** within easy reach — strong near-clinic demographic
- Students = price-sensitive, high volume general care
- Faculty = higher income, less price-sensitive

## KEY COMMERCIAL CORRIDORS

### 1. Av. Gaspar Rodríguez de Francia
- Main artery through Luque, Paseo Luque complex
- High foot traffic (families, sports, retail)
- Most premium listings
- **Gs 7–11M for 150 m²** — best visibility option

### 2. Zona CIT / Traumatocenter
- Emerging medical cluster
- Direct ortho/trauma referrals
- **Very few dental clinics here = opportunity**
- **Gs 5M+** gets equipped medical space in established health corridor
- This is the **recommended starting location** for a dental practice

### 3. Centro / Tercer Barrio / Cuarto Barrio
- Mixed residential/commercial
- Lower rent, lower visibility

### 4. Estadio Feliciano Cáceres area
- Event-driven traffic only — not consistent enough for medical practice

---

## COMPETITION IN LUQUE

- **Very few dental clinics in Luque proper** — significant market gap
- Fernando de la Mora (next city over) has the most dental concentration
- One modern multi-location brand (COP Clinic) has a Luque presence
- **No major chains** (Odontología 3, etc.) observed in Luque
- **Under-dented market:** 260k people, very few dedicated clinics

---

## SPACE TYPES AVAILABLE

### Medical Consultorios (already habilitado)
- Pre-equipped medical offices in Traumatocenter/CIT area
- Existing MSPBS habilitación = faster startup
- Usually includes: basic plumbing, electrical, waiting area
- What to ask: does this space have its own establishment habilitación?

### Commercial Retail Spaces
- More customization options
- May need to complete MSPBS habilitación process
- Better for street visibility / signage

| Priority | Location | Price | Why |
