## PURPOSE

This agreement establishes a professional referral relationship between the parties for the exchange of dental patients whose needs exceed the scope of the referring party's practice or fall within the specialized expertise of Dra. GP.

---

## SCOPE OF REFERRAL

### Cases Referred TO Dra. GP:
1. Complex restorative cases requiring advanced endodontics
2. Full-mouth rehabilitation planning
3. Second opinion consultations where treatment plan is unclear
4. Cases requiring multidisciplinary coordination (oral surgery + restorative)
5. Patients with complex medical histories requiring integrated care
6. Aesthetic cases requiring conservative treatment planning

### Cases Referred FROM Dra. GP:
1. Oral surgery cases (to trusted oral surgeon partners)
2. Periodontal specialist cases (to trusted periodontist partners)
3. Orthodontic cases (to trusted orthodontic partners)
4. Cases requiring hospitalization or general anesthesia
5. Pediatric cases beyond scope

## REFERRAL PROTOCOL

### For Referrals TO Dra. GP:

**Step 1 -- Initial Contact**
Referring party contacts Dra. GP via:
- WhatsApp: +595 XXX XXX XXX
- Email: [EMAIL]
- Phone: +595 XXX XXX XXX

**Step 2 -- Patient Information**
Referring party sends via preferred channel:
- Patient name and CI (cedula de identidad)
- Age and relevant medical history
- Radiographs (intraoral photos if available)
- Brief reason for referral
- Any relevant treatment history

**Step 3 -- Response Time**
Dra. GP commits to responding within 4 hours during business hours (8:00-18:00 Monday-Saturday).

**Step 4 -- Patient Scheduling**
Dra. GP contacts patient directly within 24 hours to schedule initial consultation.

**Step 5 -- Feedback Loop**
After patient is seen, Dra. GP sends written summary to referring dentist including:
- Diagnosis
- Treatment plan proposed
- Treatment completed (if any)
- Follow-up recommendations

### For Referrals FROM Dra. GP:

**Step 1 -- Identification of Need**
Dra. GP identifies cases requiring specialist referral during treatment planning.

**Step 2 -- Patient Discussion**
Patient is informed of referral need and reasons. Patient approval obtained.

**Step 3 -- Specialist Selection**
Dra. GP recommends appropriate specialist from her network based on:
- Case requirements
- Patient location preference
- Trust and previous experience

**Step 4 -- Coordination**
Dra. GP sends referral note including:
- Relevant clinical information
- Radiographs/images
- Treatment history summary
- Specific question or need for specialist input

**Step 5 -- Follow-up**
Dra. GP receives feedback from specialist and coordinates ongoing care.

## FEES AND COMPENSATION

### Referral Fee Structure

**Option A -- Reciprocal Referral Agreement (Preferred)**
No monetary exchange. Each party refers to the other based on case needs. Referral volume tracked and reviewed quarterly.

**Option B -- Monetary Referral Fee**
For institutional referrals (hospitals, clinicas):
- Referral fee: 15% of procedure revenue for referred patient
- Paid to referring institution quarterly
- Calculation based on completed procedures

**Option C -- Flat Fee per Referral**
For individual dentist referrals:
- Gs 50,000 per complex case referred and completed
- Paid upon treatment completion

### Fee Exclusions
- Consultations for second opinions: no referral fee
- Emergency referrals: no referral fee
- Cases that do not proceed to treatment: no referral fee

---

## MUTUAL OBLIGATIONS

### Dra. GP commits to:
1. Respond to referrals within 4 hours during business hours
2. Provide written feedback within 48 hours of patient appointment
3. Not solicit patients from referring party for personal benefit
4. Maintain clinical records for referred patients for minimum 5 years
5. Communicate any conflicts of interest regarding patient care
6. Provide annual summary of referral volume and outcomes

### Referring Party commits to:
1. Provide complete and accurate patient information with referrals
2. Not refer patients who are currently in active treatment with Dra. GP back to general care without consultation
3. Maintain patient confidentiality in all communications
4. Communicate any changes in contact information or referral protocols promptly

## CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding:
- Patient information (HIPAA-style, Ley 7593/2025 compliant)
- Business practices and pricing
- Internal communications about cases
- Any information marked as confidential

## TERM AND TERMINATION

**Term:** This agreement is valid for 12 months from the date signed, automatically renewing for successive 12-month periods unless terminated.

**Termination:** Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice. Termination does not affect patients already in treatment.

**Post-termination obligations:** Confidentiality obligations survive termination indefinitely.
