## THE OPPORTUNITY

Corporate dental benefits is a **Gs 50-200M/month market** in Asunción that goes almost entirely through insurance brokers. Most Paraguayan companies with 20+ employees pay for dental insurance through brokers who take 20-30% of the premium. The company pays full price, the broker takes a cut, and the employee gets limited coverage.

**Dra. GP can go direct.** No broker. No insurance company. Flat monthly fee per employee. Company pays less than insurance. Dra. GP earns more per patient than she would through any insurance arrangement.

```
INSURANCE BROKER CHAIN:          DIRECT CORPORATE MODEL:
Company pays premium        →    Company pays retainer
Broker keeps 20-30%         →    Dra. GP keeps 100%
Insurance processes claim   →    No claims process
Dra. GP gets 15% of value    →    Dra. GP gets 80%+ of value
Employee gets limited plan   →    Employee gets open access
```

**If 5 companies sign with 50 employees each = Gs 50M/month recurring revenue.**

## SALES SCRIPTS

### "Why not just use insurance?"
> "Insurance works, but you pay a broker 20-30% of the premium for the privilege. With us, you pay a flat fee — no broker, no claims process. Your employees get open access to a dentist who actually has time for them, not an insurance-approved dentist who has 8 minutes per patient. And your cost is 15-25% less than what you'd pay for equivalent coverage through a broker."

### "How do I know my employees will actually use it?"
> "That's the key insight. 70% of employees with dental insurance don't use it because the process is complicated and they don't know if they're covered for what they need. With us, they WhatsApp directly, they get a response within 2 hours, and they know exactly what's included. Utilization goes up. Employee satisfaction goes up. You get a healthier workforce."

### "What if an employee needs expensive work?"
> "That's exactly why the model works. Most employees need consultations and preventive care — that's included in the flat fee. When they need major work, it goes on the corporate discount rate and gets billed to the company or the employee directly. You never have to worry about catastrophic dental claims bankrupting your benefits budget."

### "Can we start with just one department?"
> "Yes. We can run a 3-month pilot with one department. At the end of 3 months, you get utilization data. If it makes sense, you expand to the whole company. If it doesn't, you walk away with no further obligation."
