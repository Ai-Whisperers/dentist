## NEGOTIATION SCENARIOS

### Scenario A - Roque Offers Luque Access (Best Case)

He says: "I can send you to Luque one day a week"
Her response: "That works. Can we formalize in writing?"

What to negotiate:
- Which day(s) in Luque - get it locked
- Does she work independently or does he send patients?
- If he sends patients - what is his cut vs hers?
- Can she bring her own patients to the Luque location?

What NOT to give up:
- Do NOT agree to exclusivity preventing her own patients
- Do NOT accept lower rate at Luque location
- Do NOT sign anything restricting independent practice

If good enough: Stay with Option A + start building private base slowly
If not enough: "I appreciate this. Can we also look at the rate? Market is Gs 100,000+"

### Scenario B - Roque Offers More Money Only

He says: "I will raise you to Gs 80,000 per restoration. Stay."
Her response: "I appreciate that. Is there any path to Luque? My concern is not just the rate."

Evaluate:
- Is Gs 80,000 raise enough? (Gs 2M/month more = Gs 24M/year)
- Does he still control schedule/location/patient flow?
- Can she build private practice on the side if he allows it?

Danger: He may try to lock her in with contract restricting private work.

She requires in writing:
- Explicit: she CAN practice privately in her own time
- Explicit: NO non-compete
- If he will not put in writing -> WALK AWAY

### Scenario C - Roque Says No to Everything

He says: "No to Luque. No to rate increase. Contract stays as is."
Her response: "I understand. I will be starting my own practice. I wanted you to know directly."

Do NOT:
- Get angry
- Threaten or negotiate
- Accept "last chance" offers after meeting

Why: Decision is made. Move to Option B immediately.

Same day/next day:
1. Start E.A.S. registration (eas.mic.gov.py)
2. Contact 3 Luque spaces from shortlist
3. Set up WhatsApp Business
4. Message first contacts (trusted, likely to book)

### Scenario D - Roque Gets Defensive or Hostile

He says: "You cannot work elsewhere. The contract prevents it."
Her response: "I understand. I will have my lawyer review the contract."
Then walk away.

Do NOT:
- Argue
- Negotiate under pressure
- Accept verbal threats as real

Why: Verbal threats in meeting are not contract clauses.
Next: Lawyer reviews contract within 48 hours.
