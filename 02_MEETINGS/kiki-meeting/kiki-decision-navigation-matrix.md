## SCENARIO 1: ROQUE OFFERS REAL IMPROVEMENT

### What "Real Improvement" Means:
- % payout increases by 20%+ (e.g., from 12% to 15%+)
- OR fixed fee per procedure that equals Gs 300k+ per restoration
- OR allows private practice on evenings/weekends
- OR offers equipped space in Luque at reasonable price

### Decision Matrix:

| Improvement Level | Action | Rationale |
|------------------|--------|-----------|
| **Excellent** (>30% better) | Option A + Negotiate written agreement | Keep day job, test premium market |
| **Good** (20-30% better) | Option A cautiously + start Option B in parallel | Hedge bets |
| **Token** (5-15% better) | Evaluate if truly better than current | May not be worth the complexity |

### Option A: Premium Upsell (If Roque Allows)

**What works within current clinic:**
- Second opinion sessions: Gs 400-600k
- Treatment planning sessions: Gs 500-800k
- Complex case consultations: Gs 300-500k

**Requirements to make Option A work:**
1. Roque's explicit written permission
2. Clear terms (does he take % of private sessions?)
3. She keeps 100% of planning fees OR negotiated split
4. No conflict with her contract

**Financial projection (Option A alone):**
```
Month 1-6: +Gs 15-30M additional income
Risk: Low (keeps current income)
Investment: Zero
```

### Implementation of Option A:
- [ ] Negotiate written agreement with Roque
- [ ] Set up separate billing for planning sessions
- [ ] Market to existing patients: "Comprehensive planning sessions available"
- [ ] Track conversion rate
