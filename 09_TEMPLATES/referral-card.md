## USAGE

1. Give this card to patient after successful treatment
2. Say verbally: "Si conocés a alguien que necesite un dentista, pasale esta tarjeta. Los dos reciben crédito."
3. When referred person contacts you: ask "¿Quién te recomendó?" → log in CRM
4. When referred person completes treatment: activate referrer's credit
