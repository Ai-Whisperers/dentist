Oral Rehabilitation: when the mouth needs a plan, not patches

It's the full reconstruction of a mouth with much to resolve. Missing teeth, old failed restorations, accumulated work. It's not "fixing one tooth." It's looking at the whole mouth, understanding what happened, and building a plan that's clinically, functionally, and economically sound.

Who this is for

Patients with multiple missing teeth, failed treatments, accumulated work, old crowns, chronic pain nobody could explain. Patients who went from office to office without finding a solution.

How I work

1. I listen. 2. I explain with the panoramic X-ray on screen. 3. We plan together. 4. I execute with clinical judgment. 5. I refer when appropriate.

What it's not

It's not "veneers for everyone." It's not "fix what you can and leave the rest." It's not "the most expensive plan." If what you need is something conservative, I'll tell you.

Second opinion: decide with real information

If another professional told you that you need important treatment and you want to confirm, bring the quote. I'll give you a formal second opinion, all in writing.

When to ask for one

You were told you need endodontics, a crown, an extraction, or a large treatment plan. You were quoted something that seems expensive. You already started a treatment and want to confirm.

What's included

Analysis of previous X-rays, full clinical exam, review of the proposed plan, alternative plan documented in writing, referral to a specialist if appropriate.

Confidentiality

What we discuss is strictly between you and me. If you decide not to proceed with my proposal, I deliver all the documentation in writing. I never speak poorly of a colleague: I don't know their full context.
