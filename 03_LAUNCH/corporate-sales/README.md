## Programs

### Corporate Dental Benefits (3 Tiers)

| Tier | Employees | Rate/Employee/Month | Includes |
|------|-----------|---------------------|----------|
| **Tier 1** — Empresa Chica | 5–20 | Gs 150,000 | 2 consultations, 4 discounted checkups, 1 cleaning |
| **Tier 2** — Empresa Mediana | 21–100 | Gs 130,000 | 4 consultations, 6 checkups, 2 cleanings, HR reports |
| **Tier 3** — Empresa Grande | 100+ | Gs 110,000 | 6 consultations, unlimited 2nd opinions, on-site days |

All tiers: 12-month minimum, family add-ons available, corporate discounts on procedures.

### Sports Club Partnership

Zero-cost benefit for clubs: members get 20% off all dental services. Club does nothing except share a flyer. Dra. GP tracks usage and sends quarterly reports.
