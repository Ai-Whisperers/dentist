# WEBSITE ADDITION — "When Not to Treat" Section
## Dra. Gabriella González Pane — Premium Dental Practice
**Priority:** HIGH
**Version:** 1.0 — June 2026

---

## PURPOSE & CONVERSION GOAL

**Job:** Differentiate the practice through radical transparency. This section explicitly states the cases and situations where Dra. GP does NOT accept patients. This builds trust faster than any positive claim because it proves she's not desperate for patients and she has clear boundaries.

**Conversion goal:** Filter out bad-fit patients before they contact, reducing wasted consultations and increasing the quality of every patient relationship.

---

## TARGET AUDIENCE

1. **Skeptical patients** — who've been burned by overtreatment elsewhere
2. **Complex cases** — who need specialist care and should be referred
3. **Emergency-only seekers** — who want quick fixes without planning
4. **Price-shoppers** — looking for the cheapest option, not quality care
