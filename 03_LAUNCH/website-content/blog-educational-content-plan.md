## BLOG POST PLAN (5 POSTS TO START)

### Post 1: "5 Signs Your Dentist Might Be Overtreating You"
**Word count:** 1,200-1,500 words
**Target keyword:** dentist overtreatment Paraguay

**Content structure:**
- Hook: Relatable scenario ("You went in for a cleaning. You left with a treatment plan for 4 crowns...")
- Body: 5 signs with explanations
- Conclusion: "If you recognize these signs, consider getting a second opinion."
- CTA: "Book a second opinion consultation"

### Post 2: "What Is a Second Opinion in Dentistry?"
**Word count:** 800-1,000 words
**Target keyword:** dental second opinion Asunción

**Content structure:**
- Hook: "The question isn't whether you NEED the dental work. The question is whether you need ALL OF IT."
- Body: What a second opinion is, what happens during visit, what you receive
- Examples of cases where second opinions revealed unnecessary work
- CTA: "Bring your X-rays. Get honest assessment."

### Post 3: "Root Canal vs. Extraction: What Dentists Don't Always Tell You"
**Word count:** 1,000-1,200 words
**Target keyword:** root canal vs extraction Paraguay

**Content structure:**
- When root canal is the right choice
- When extraction is the right choice
- Questions to ask before deciding
- What happens after extraction options

### Post 4: "Why Do Dental Prices Vary So Much in Asunción?"
**Word count:** 900-1,100 words
**Target keyword:** dentist price Asunción

**Content structure:**
- Why dental prices vary (materials, skill, lab work, overhead)
- What makes a price "too low" vs "too high"
- Questions to evaluate if a price is fair

### Post 5: "What to Expect at Your First Dental Visit in Paraguay"
**Word count:** 700-900 words
**Target keyword:** dentist Asunción first visit

**Content structure:**
- Step-by-step from patient perspective
- Duration, what happens, what you receive
- No pressure to proceed
